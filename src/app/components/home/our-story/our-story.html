Our History and Origins

The genesis of ExpediNap lies in a vocation for service that has evolved over the years. Beginning their professional careers in the vibrant tourist areas of Punta Cana and Bávaro, they acquired valuable experience in diverse fields.


From civil aviation security at Punta Cana International Airport to passenger service at renowned airlines such as Frontier Airlines and reception roles at renowned hotels such as Meliá Caribe and Now Onyx.


The 2019 pandemic became a catalyst for transformation. It was a time of introspection and growth, prompting the decision to acquire web programming skills at the Technological Institute of the Americas (ITLA). The goal: to create their own platform that would offer quality services and foster a collaborative environment.

Mission

To position ExpediNap as the top choice for travelers seeking authentic and exciting experiences in the Dominican Republic. To offer a comprehensive platform for booking excursions, luxury yacht charters, and transportation services, ensuring that every adventure is memorable.

Vision

To be recognized as the leading tourism services company in the Dominican Republic, distinguished by excellence in customer service, the breadth of its offerings, and its commitment to traveler satisfaction in each of its destinations, whether by land, sea, or even underwater.
